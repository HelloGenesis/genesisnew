The process below is the one we actually run. It is written down because
briefs go wrong in predictable places, and most of them are early.

## 1. The brief argument

Before anything is made, we argue about the brief. Who is this for, what
should change in their head, and how will we know. A brief that survives this
is worth producing against.

## 2. Direction, then casting

The creative direction comes first; creators are matched to it. Doing this in
the other order is how brands end up with a campaign shaped by whoever was
available.

## 3. Production

Scripting, shoot or generation, edit. AI carries the variants; the hero cut
gets human attention start to finish.

## 4. Publish and read the numbers

The metrics were agreed in step one, so this step is arithmetic rather than
argument.
