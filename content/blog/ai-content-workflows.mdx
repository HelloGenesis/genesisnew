Every agency now claims an AI workflow. Most mean they generated a moodboard.
Here is what actually changed for us, and what did not.

## What it replaced

**Variant production.** A campaign needs the same idea in nine aspect ratios,
four languages and three lengths. That used to be a week of nobody's favourite
work. It is now an afternoon.

**First-pass concepting.** Twenty directions on screen in an hour means the
argument about which one is right happens earlier, with something to point at.

**Stock and placeholder.** Rough cuts no longer wait on a library search.

## What it did not replace

**The idea.** A model will give you the average of everything it has seen.
Average is precisely what a campaign cannot be.

**The edit.** Timing is taste. Nothing has come close.

**Accountability.** Someone signs off. That someone is a person.

## The honest trade

AI moved our bottleneck. It did not remove it. The constraint used to be
production hours; now it is judgement: how fast a human can look at forty
options and know which two are worth finishing.

That is a better problem to have. It is still a problem.
